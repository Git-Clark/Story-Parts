---
title: "Against the word visionary"
date: "2026-05-24"
topic: "criticism"
tint: "effects"
dek: "It has one job in film writing, which is to end an argument before it starts."
---

The word does no descriptive work. It cannot be falsified, it cannot be compared, and it has no content beyond approval.

What it does do is close a question. Once a director is a visionary, the vision is theirs by definition, and asking who designed the thing you are looking at becomes a slightly rude question about implementation.

The test is simple: remove the word from a sentence and see whether the sentence still claims anything. Usually it does not.
